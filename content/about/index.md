Jeff Geiser is VP, Customer Engineering at [Zenlayer](https://zenlayer.com),
focused on enterprise AI and sovereign inference infrastructure, based in
Northern Virginia.

He helps large organizations build training and inference infrastructure,
particularly at the edge. The conviction behind the rest of his work is
simple: inference infrastructure people run themselves deserves the same
rigor cloud infrastructure has had for a decade.

That splits into two problems he builds on. First, measuring and routing a
sovereign stack: [Wicklee](https://wicklee.dev/) gives real cost per million
tokens and tokens-per-watt across your own GPUs.. how a system decides where
inference should run, local or frontier, given what each node can actually do
right now — is one I write about as the decision loop. Second, proving a
sovereign stack is actually sovereign:
[bordercheck](https://github.com/jeffgeiser/bordercheck) tests whether your
data stays inside the border you drew by stressing the real stack and
inspecting everywhere data lands.. no leaks!

Also: ongoing research into small private enterprise models (a fine-tuned
Qwen2.5-7B built to run on owned hardware), writing on sovereign inference
infrastructure, and base-building for running.
