### [Wicklee](https://wicklee.dev/)
Hardware-first observability and cost governance for self-hosted inference.
A WES efficiency score (tokens per watt) and real cost per million tokens
across your own GPUs. Free community tier.

### [bordercheck](https://github.com/jeffgeiser/bordercheck)
A test harness that checks whether data in your AI stack actually stays
inside the border you drew. It runs a canary through the real stack, stresses
it to force failure and fallback behavior, then inspects every layer data
lands in (logs, caches, vector stores, egress) and hands you audit-grade
evidence. Residency proven by behavior, not by diagram. The first tool in a
proof layer for sovereign AI.

### [elm-research](https://github.com/jeffgeiser/elm-research)
An open methodology (not a commercial product) for training small private
enterprise models. Current work: `account-intelligence-7b-v1`, a fine-tune of
Qwen2.5-7B for enterprise account briefs, built to run on owned hardware and
emit structured signals rather than prose, as a component in compound-AI
pipelines.

### Zenlayer Central
An internal agentic enterprise platform running Qwen2.5-32B-FP8 on a DGX Spark
alongside Claude, generating account intelligence, meeting prep, and pricing
assistance for sales teams via integrations with Salesforce, Confluence, and
other internal systems.
